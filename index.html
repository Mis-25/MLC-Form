📋 Registration Rules

1

Graduate eligibility: You must have completed graduation (or an equivalent qualification) from a recognised university on or before 2022.

2

Residence: You should be an ordinary resident of the constituency. Fill your full current address correctly with PIN code.

3

Address proof (mandatory): Aadhaar card, electricity bill, telephone bill, rent agreement or any other valid address proof — clear copy only.

4

Graduation proof (mandatory): Final year marksheet, degree certificate or provisional degree. The document must clearly show your name and year of passing.

5

Photograph (mandatory): One recent passport-size photo with a plain background. Selfies with filters or group photos are not accepted.

6

File rules: Only PDF, JPG or PNG files up to 10 MB. Every file is automatically checked — invalid or duplicate files are rejected.

7

One registration per person: Submit only once. Duplicate or false submissions may lead to rejection of your application.

8

Correct details: Name and details must match your Aadhaar. Wrong or unclear documents will require re-submission.

9

Declaration: By submitting, you confirm that all documents belong to you and the information given is true.

Keep all three documents ready before starting. The form can be submitted only when every step is complete.
